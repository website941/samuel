# 02. Digital Services Catalogue & Technical Delivery
## Marks and Spencer Group plc

### 1. Online Fashion & Homeware Shopping
- **Category:** E-Commerce Retail
- **Target Audience:** UK retail shoppers and international delivery customers across 100+ markets.
- **Description:** Shop thousands of lines across womenswear, menswear, lingerie, childrenswear, and home furnishings with comprehensive size guides, video catwalk clips, and real customer fit ratings.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Next.js / React micro-frontends backed by headless commerce APIs, image optimization edge workers, and dynamic inventory queries.

### 2. M&S Food to Order & Entertaining Collection
- **Category:** Foodhall & Catering
- **Target Audience:** Families celebrating weddings, birthdays, Christmas, and dinner parties.
- **Description:** Order bespoke celebration cakes, party platters, holiday turkeys, and artisan cheese boards online for designated collection slots at your local M&S Foodhall.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Specialist bakery and cold-chain ordering engine enforcing strict slot reservation capacities per store location.

### 3. Sparks Digital Loyalty & Personalized Offers
- **Category:** Customer Loyalty
- **Target Audience:** Over 16 million registered Sparks members across the UK.
- **Description:** Scan your Sparks card or mobile app every time you shop to unlock tailored personalized discounts, charity donations on your behalf, and chances to win your basket for free.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Machine learning recommendation engines on Google Cloud Platform analyzing purchasing histories to serve hyper-personalized offer tokens.

### 4. Free Next-Day Click & Collect & Store Stock Finder
- **Category:** Omnichannel Services
- **Target Audience:** Busy high-street shoppers avoiding postal delivery wait times.
- **Description:** Order online by 8pm for free collection next day at any of 1,000+ M&S stores or local BP convenience outlets; check real-time aisle stock before visiting.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Distributed real-time stock lookup APIs querying SAP S/4HANA enterprise retail inventory databases in sub-100ms.

### 5. Hassle-Free Returns & QR Code Drop-Off
- **Category:** Post-Purchase Care
- **Target Audience:** Online customers needing size exchanges or refund processing.
- **Description:** Generate instant paperless QR return codes to drop parcels at any M&S store, Post Office, or Royal Mail parcel locker with automated refunds issued to original payment methods.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Integration with Royal Mail returns API and automated reverse logistics warehouse tracking.

### 6. M&S Bank & Financial Services Integration
- **Category:** Banking & Financial
- **Target Audience:** UK consumers seeking trusted financial products with Sparks points benefits.
- **Description:** Apply for M&S Credit Cards, Club Rewards, travel insurance, pet insurance, and foreign currency with direct bureau collection in store.
- **Technical Delivery Pipeline:** Secure Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) compliant banking gateway with Open Banking API integration and biometric MFA.
