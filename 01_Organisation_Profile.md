# 01. Organisation Profile & Statutory Constitution
## Marks and Spencer Group plc (Marks & Spencer)

- **Sector:** UK Retail, Food, Clothing, Home & Financial Services
- **Founded:** 1884 (Founded by Michael Marks and Thomas Spencer in Leeds Kirkgate Market)
- **Headquarters:** Waterside House, 35 North Wharf Road, London W2 1NW
- **Regulator:** Competition and Markets Authority (CMA), Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) & Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)
- **Statutory Basis & Governance:** Board of Directors, Marks and Spencer Group plc, regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA for M&S Bank)
- **Tagline:** "Quality, Value, Service, Innovation and Trust Since 1884"

### Executive Mission Overview
Marks and Spencer (M&S) is one of the United Kingdom’s most iconic retailers, renowned for premium food halls, responsibly sourced fashion, stylish homeware, and M&S Bank. Its high-traffic omnichannel e-commerce platform processes tens of millions of retail orders annually, synchronising nationwide stock across 1,000+ British stores and automated distribution centres.
